Acorn Counter is designed so children and parents can use it with peace of mind: we handle only the information the app needs, in ways that are easy to understand.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the Acorn Counter app and its support pages (together, the "Service").

2. Information the app handles

The current first version does not ask you to register or log in. The main information the app handles is:

  • Images taken with the camera
  • The number of acorns recognized in those images
  • Your counts and totals, stored on your device

The app has no feature that collects your name, address, phone number, email address, or similar personal information.

3. Photos and recognition

Acorn recognition in captured images runs entirely on your device. In the current version, captured images are never sent to an external server for recognition.

A captured image is held only temporarily, while you check the recognition result, and is discarded when you confirm or cancel. Images are not automatically saved to your device's photo library.

4. How information is used

Information is used only to count acorns, to display your counts and totals, and to provide the app experience. The app does not currently use any third-party analytics SDKs for advertising or behavioral analysis.

5. Storage and sharing

Your counts and totals are stored on your device. The first version does not sync anything to the cloud.

We do not provide information handled by the app to third parties, except where required by law.

6. About these support pages

These support pages are delivered via GitHub Pages. Access logs and similar data needed to serve the pages may be handled in accordance with GitHub's policies. For details, please see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or how information is handled, please contact us at nariyukipdm@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as features are added or laws change. If an important change is made, we will note it by updating the date on this page.
